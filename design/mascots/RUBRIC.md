# Mascot rubric

Scored by a reviewer who sees only the rendered screenshots (several animation times). Each criterion is PASS or FAIL
with one concrete fix when it fails. A mascot passes the loop only when every criterion passes.

For each of the two candidates (Glim the firefly, Hoot the owl), across all five moods:

1. **Species at a glance.** Someone who has never seen it says "firefly" / "owl" within a second, without the caption.
2. **Moods are distinguishable without text.** calm, waiting, annoyed, angry and done look clearly different from
   each other. Calm vs waiting is the hard pair and must differ by more than a blink.
3. **The face reads.** Eyes have contrast against what is behind them and the expression (relaxed, alert, unimpressed,
   furious, delighted) is legible at 64px, not only at 256px.
4. **Clean pixel art.** One consistent 1px plum outline, one light direction (top left), a small palette, no stray
   or noisy pixels, no accidental gaps in outlines, dithering only where it is meant (the firefly halo).
5. **Appeal.** Cute and friendly, in the same family as Mochi (soft highlights, plum outline, chunky shapes). Not creepy,
   not a different art style.
6. **Fits the 32x32 frame.** Nothing is clipped at the top, sides or bottom in any mood or animation time.
7. **Animation reads.** Across the frames provided, the motion supports the mood (the firefly's glow pulses faster as
   it gets more urgent; the owl blinks, taps, ruffles). Frames of one mood stay recognisably the same character.
8. **Works as an alert.** Waiting, annoyed and angry escalate visibly; done feels like a reward.

Also report, for each candidate, the single change that would most improve it.
